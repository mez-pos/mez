# UI/UX audit and fix plan, 2026-10-31

An audit of every mez screen, run with the impeccable skill (`audit` for
the technical checks, `critique` for the UX review). Nothing in the code
was changed by it. This file is the plan for fixing what it found.

## How it was done

- **Build:** v0.2.0-beta.1 (`dist/mez_v0.2.0-beta.1_windows_amd64.exe`,
  commit 58e376d8), run against a throwaway demo venue ("Corner Cafe",
  `mez setup -demo-menu`, GST India, prices inclusive).
- **Walked in a browser by script**, as cashier, manager, waiter, cook,
  owner and guest: lock, open shift, register, item choices, tender (cash,
  card, UPI, split, discount), invoice, print queue, tables, order
  history, refund, close of day, waiter home and order, kitchen display,
  all eleven back-office screens with their dialogs, the guest app on a
  phone, and the setup wizard on an empty server.
- **Sizes**, from the venue's devices (`docs/00-product.md`): 1366x768 (counter PC), 1024x768 (tablet),
  1920x1080, and 390x844 (guest phone). 164 screenshots.
- **Reviewed** by six independent reviewers (till selling path; tables,
  history, close and waiter; kitchen display; back office; guest and
  setup; code-level technical audit). Each finding was checked against
  the source; the two P0s were checked twice.
- **Detector:** `impeccable detect` over `web/` found 3 pattern hits (see
  "Rejected and false positives").
- The screenshots and capture scripts are in this session's scratchpad
  and are not kept. Re-run the walk to see a fix in place.

## What is good, and should stay

- **Money paths are careful.** Idempotency keys on every payment and on
  guest orders, "Sending" states, tips kept apart, a refund that asks for
  a manager's PIN up front, and a blind drawer count.
- **The structure is accessible.** All ~440 buttons are real buttons,
  every icon-only button has a label, dialogs are native `<dialog>` with
  focus return, and no raw hex exists outside `tokens.css`.
- **Status is a word almost everywhere**, and errors are written in plain
  venue language. The setup wizard's inline validation is excellent.
- **Terminal touch targets meet 48px**; the guest bundle is 47.9 of
  60 KB.

## The two P0s

1. **A bill paid in full stays "Open" until the cashier taps "New Order"
   (TBS-1).** A paid order stays `settling` until an `order.closed`
   event, and the only close on the till is the paid panel's New Order
   button. Leave the paid screen any other way and Order History shows
   the paid invoice as "Open" under "Paid (0)", and the waiter's tablet
   calls the table "Needs Bill". A guest can be asked to pay twice.
2. **A counter order can be paid and closed without ever reaching the
   kitchen (TILL-1).** Pay navigates straight to the tender screen
   (`OrderScreen.tsx:208-210`); nothing checks for unsent lines on Pay,
   the tender screen or New Order; the server's close checks only the
   balance (`internal/domain/order/settle.go`). Ring up, Pay, take cash,
   New Order: the guest has paid for food nobody was told to make.

## Root causes behind most findings

1. **768px tall was never designed for.** The `short:` variant is used in
   5 of 242 files. Rails, tender cards, kitchen columns, drawer rows and
   modal footers lose their working content first at 1366x768 and
   1024x768, the venue's real sizes.
2. **Fixed widths in `.tsx` files** (16 side columns, fixed table grids)
   against tokens.md's rule. At 1024 the middle of the screen collapses
   while an empty rail keeps its width.
3. **Three token pairings fail contrast.** `--muted` is 4.47:1 on
   parchment (not the 4.6:1 tokens.md states) and 4.13:1 on wells;
   `--faint` is 3.5:1; terracotta on its own tint is 3.96:1. Fixing three
   tokens clears most AA failures in all four apps.
4. **Server lifecycle and ids leak into staff words.** `settling` becomes
   "Open" and "Needs Bill"; orders are named `ord_9VSDSDVQ`; "Orders"
   names three different places.
5. **Time is computed three ways** (venue zone, browser zone, UTC), with
   a hard-coded 04:00 day start in the back office.
6. **Shared primitives, unshared policy.** Sheet and Modal dismiss
   differently, buttons use two focus rings, back-office fields do not
   link their hints and errors, and drafts are dropped silently on some
   screens and guarded on others.
7. **Decoration on working screens.** Script lines, taglines and stats on
   the kitchen board and waiter home, against tokens.md's "decoration is
   for screens that wait".

## Fix plan

Tasks in order. Each is sized for one session under the project's
protocol (one task, `make check`, STATE.md, commit). It can be filed
as a phase file as it stands. A task touching `web/shared` also changes
mez-cloud's account area (STATE.md): build it there too. Items marked
**ADR** overturn a recorded decision and need the case written in
DECISIONS.md first.
